# Location photo credits

These are real, freely reusable photographs from Wikimedia Commons.
Before/after are illustrative game labels, not evidence that the same scene was cleaned or restored. Photographs use different dates and viewpoints. Visible clarity alone does not establish water or air quality.
For Gangotri, the comparison illustrates glacier melt and the snowy Mount Shivling landscape near Gangotri as a conservation goal. It does not show reversal of glacier retreat; natural debris is not pollution.

All files were proportionally resized and converted to PNG; no scene content or colours were changed. Each derivative keeps the source photo license listed below. Keep this file with any redistribution of the photos.
